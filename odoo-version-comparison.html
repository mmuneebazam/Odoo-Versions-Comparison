6 · Which Version Is Better — For Which Purpose?

A practical decision guide. Community edition is free (LGPL) on every version; Enterprise adds Studio, the mobile app, Barcode, IoT, advanced accounting and official support, priced per user.

Odoo 20
New implementation starting now
Longest support runway (~Sep 2029), agentic AI, offline mode, headless REST/GraphQL. First verify the modules you need have a 20.0 build — if not, start on 19.
Odoo 19
Stability & proven ecosystem first
Supported until Sep 2028, mature module/partner ecosystem, native AI already in place, ~30% faster than v18. The safest modern target for most upgrades today.
Odoo 19 / 20 Community
Tight budget — Community (free) edition
Community is free (LGPL) on any version — but never deploy 15–17: they no longer receive security fixes. Use the newest Community you can staff.
Odoo 20
Manufacturing & warehouses
Continuous production, weekly Kanban MRP, CMR documents, smarter reordering. Fall back to 18/19 if your shop-floor custom modules aren't ported yet.
Odoo 20 (or 19)
Retail, POS & field teams
v20 adds true offline work with sync and multi-day offline POS. v19 already has the revamped POS with dark mode and order presets.
Odoo 19 / 20
eCommerce & websites
v19: SEO assistant, faster shop, marketplace connectors. v20: AI builds pages, forms and product content from a prompt.

7 · Upgrade Path

Wherever you are today, here is the recommended move.

✨ The Bottom Line

  • Never start a new project on Odoo 15, 16 or 17 — they are at or past end of support and no longer receive security fixes.
  • Starting fresh today → Odoo 20 (released 24 Sep 2026): longest support runway, agentic AI, offline mode, native REST/GraphQL. Confirm your must-have modules exist on 20.0; otherwise Odoo 19 is the equally valid, more proven choice (supported to Sep 2028).
  • On Odoo 17 → upgrade NOW (support ends this month, Sep 2026). Target 19 for safety or 20 for features — you can skip versions.
  • On Odoo 18 → plan the upgrade within 12 months (support to Sep 2027).
  • On Odoo 15/16 → urgent migration, jumping straight to 19 or 20; budget for custom-module porting — the older your base, the more compatibility work.
  • Rule of thumb: stay within the three most recent majors (today: 18, 19, 20) so you keep security patches, module availability and vendor support. Odoo 20 is the future; Odoo 19 is the safe present.
